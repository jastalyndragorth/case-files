CASE 002 • MARLOW'S REST MOTEL • PRIORITY: REVIEW

A guest checked into Room 8 of a small roadside motel on a rainy Thursday night. By morning, he was gone.

His car remained in the parking lot. His phone, wallet, jacket, and overnight bag were still inside the room. The night manager says the deadbolt was engaged when staff opened the door with an emergency key.

Complication: motel records show another guest vanished from Room 8 thirty-two years earlier. That person's belongings were also left behind.

Hunter Objective: determine what happened to the missing guest, identify the supernatural threat if one exists, and explain why two disappearances separated by decades appear connected to the same room.

Evidence

SCENE MATERIAL • ROOM 8
ITEM A

Guest Belongings

Wallet, phone, keys, shoes, jacket, and a packed overnight bag were found inside. Nothing obvious appears stolen.

ITEM B

Security Footage

At 2:13 A.M., a man matching the missing guest walks past the lobby camera toward the parking lot. The image is grainy. For two frames, his eyes reflect unusually bright.

ITEM C

Bathroom Drain

Housekeeping found several short strands of hair and a small amount of translucent material caught beneath the drain cover. Staff assumed it was soap residue and did not clean it after police sealed the room.

ITEM D

Room Door

No damage was found on the door or frame. The inside deadbolt was engaged. The bathroom window is narrow and painted shut.

Witness Statements

INTERVIEW EXTRACTS

Mara Voss — Night Manager

Voss checked the guest in at 9:46 P.M. She remembers him as tired but friendly. Near midnight she heard two male voices arguing in the hallway, but when she looked out, only the guest from Room 8 was visible.

Caleb Trent — Guest, Room 10

Trent says he saw the missing guest at approximately 1:30 A.M. near the ice machine. He was barefoot and wearing a gray T-shirt. Trent remembers thinking the man's expression looked “wrong somehow,” but cannot explain why.

Renee Vale — Sister of Missing Guest

Vale confirms her brother called her at 12:08 A.M. He complained that somebody at the motel had knocked twice and then stood silently outside his door. He sounded irritated, not frightened. She also notes that he never walks barefoot outside a hotel room because of an old ankle injury.

Locations

FIELD MAP NOTES

Marlow's Rest Motel

A two-story, twenty-room roadside motel operating since the late 1960s. Several renovations have changed the lobby and exterior, but Room 8 remains in its original location.

Room 8

Second floor, end of the west corridor. One door to the hallway, one small bathroom window, and no connecting door. A maintenance access space runs behind the bathroom wall but is sealed from the guest room.

Old Service Building

A disused laundry and boiler structure stands behind the west wing. It was closed after the motel renovation twenty-seven years ago and is now used for storage.

Documents

ARCHIVE EXTRACTS

1984 Missing Person Notice

Daniel Mercer, age 29. Last confirmed location: Room 8, Marlow's Rest. Vehicle and personal belongings recovered at motel. A clerk reported seeing Mercer leave the west stairwell shortly after 3:00 A.M., but another witness claimed to hear Mercer speaking inside Room 8 almost twenty minutes later.

1999 Renovation Invoice

Workers repairing the west wing recorded “organic debris” inside the wall cavity behind Rooms 7–9. The contractor attributed it to rodents and removed only what blocked new plumbing.

Local Folklore Column — 2007

A newspaper Halloween feature calls Room 8 “the room that keeps its guests.” The article claims Daniel Mercer's ghost locks the door after luring travelers inside. No written version of that legend has been located before 2007.

New Leads

RESEARCH PRIORITIES
  1. Compare the 1984 witness timeline with the current security footage. In both cases, one piece of evidence places the missing guest outside while another suggests the guest remained inside.
  2. Do not assume the Room 8 ghost story explains the first disappearance. The published legend appears more than twenty years after Daniel Mercer vanished.
  3. Consult the Bestiary for creatures associated with identity copying, stolen memories, unusual camera-eye effects, discarded biological material, and impersonation.
  4. Ask whether the locked-room problem requires the missing guest to have left the room at all.

THREAT IDENTIFIED

SHAPESHIFTER

The missing guest did not walk out of Room 8. Something wearing his face did.

What Happened

A Shapeshifter using the abandoned service spaces behind the west wing entered the maintenance cavity adjoining Room 8. After copying the guest, it left the room in his appearance. The original victim was taken through the concealed service route rather than through the locked hallway door.

Why the Evidence Fits

  • Lobby footage: the person leaving looked like the guest, while the unusual eye reflection supports Shapeshifter lore.
  • Bathroom material: hair and translucent residue are consistent with biological material left after a transformation.
  • Barefoot sighting: the copy reproduced appearance and memory well enough to pass casually, but missed a small personal habit tied to the real guest's ankle injury.
  • Two voices: the guest and his duplicate were both present before the disappearance.
  • 1984 contradiction: Daniel Mercer was reportedly outside and still heard inside because the same type of impersonation occurred then.
  • 2007 ghost legend: it is a later explanation attached to an older mystery, making it a persuasive red herring rather than the cause.

Weakness

Silver is the defining supernatural weakness of an ordinary Shapeshifter. The binder records the canon rule without practical weapon-use guidance.
