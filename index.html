Case 001 • Status: Active

The Voices on Briar Road

Field Review

Initial Field Briefing

Case: 001
Location: Briar Road, rural Indiana
Priority: Review
Status: Active

Over the last six weeks, three unrelated people have reported unusual activity near an abandoned farmhouse on Briar Road. Each witness entered or approached the property after believing someone they knew was calling to them from inside.

No person matching those voices was found at the scene. Two witnesses fled without injury. The third disappeared for several hours and was later found disoriented nearly a mile from the property.

Local residents attribute the incidents to a death that occurred at the farmhouse decades ago. Authorities consider the reports trespassing incidents complicated by local folklore.

Hunter Objective: Determine what is happening at the Briar Road property, identify the nature of the threat if one exists, and explain the contradictory evidence.

Evidence Item A — Doorframe

Investigators photographed several fresh scratches along the inside frame of the farmhouse's rear door. The marks are irregular and do not form recognizable writing or symbols.

Evidence Item B — Temperature Log

A volunteer searcher noted that the main hallway felt unusually cold during the third incident. Exterior temperature that evening was 46°F. No working heat or electricity exists at the property.

Evidence Item C — Phone Recording

Witness Two captured eleven seconds of audio while standing outside the house. A faint human voice can be heard saying, “Come around back. I found it.” The witness identified the voice as her older brother.

Her brother was confirmed to be approximately forty miles away at the time.

Witness Statement — Nora Bell

Nora, 34, stopped near the property after hearing what she believed was her mother's voice calling her childhood nickname from the porch. Nora's mother lives in another state. Nora approached the house but left after the voice moved from the porch to somewhere behind her without Nora seeing anyone cross the yard.

Witness Statement — Elise Warren

Elise, 27, went to the farmhouse with two friends after hearing local ghost stories. While outside, she heard her brother's voice from inside telling her to come around back. Her friends heard a voice but could not identify it. Elise made the phone recording found in Evidence Item C.

Witness Statement — Marcus Reed

Marcus, 41, was reported missing after his truck was found near Briar Road. Searchers located him several hours later walking beside a drainage ditch. Marcus remembers hearing his deceased father's voice inside the farmhouse but has little memory of what happened afterward.

Briar Road Farmhouse

Vacant for approximately twenty-eight years. The structure sits beyond a long gravel drive surrounded by neglected fields and tree line. The rear of the property opens toward several acres of overgrown woodland.

A former resident, Evelyn Price, died at the property many years before it was abandoned. Local versions of the story disagree about the circumstances, but the death is the source of the area's “Briar Road Ghost” legend.

Search Area

Marcus Reed was found northwest of the farmhouse, beyond the tree line. Searchers reported no clear reason he would have traveled in that direction.

Archive Extract — County Historical Notes

The earliest written reference found to strange voices near Briar Road predates the farmhouse's abandonment. A 1981 letter to a local newspaper describes a teenager claiming that he heard his sister calling from the woods behind the property even though she was at home.

The letter was printed as part of a Halloween folklore column and was not investigated.

Audio Follow-Up

Elise Warren's brother listened to the recovered recording. He agreed that the voice sounded similar to his own, but stated that “Come around back. I found it” is not a phrase he recognizes saying to Elise and has no special meaning between them.

Research Lead 01

Compare the chronology of the local ghost legend with the earliest reported voice incident.

Research Lead 02

Consider whether all reported phenomena must have the same cause. An abandoned unheated building, local folklore, and a genuine supernatural threat can produce overlapping evidence.

Research Lead 03

Consult the Bestiary for phenomena associated with imitation, familiar voices, luring behavior, altered perception, and identity deception. Do not select an identification based on a single matching sign.
