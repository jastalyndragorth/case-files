Initial Field Briefing

CASE 005 • ACTIVE INVESTIGATION

At 12:03 A.M., local radio station WCRK abruptly interrupted its overnight music program with the voice of retired host Daniel Cross—who died eight months ago.

The voice read the names of three living residents and said, “You already know what you did.” By sunrise, one of those residents had disappeared.

Police believe someone used archived recordings to stage a threat. Station staff disagree: the message included a private phrase Cross used off-air and referenced an event that was never broadcast.

Hunter Objective: Determine who created the broadcast, why three people were named, and whether the missing resident is victim, target, or participant.

Evidence

RECOVERED MATERIAL

Item A — Broadcast File

The station's automation system contains no scheduled file matching the midnight interruption. The recording exists only in the outgoing transmission archive.

Item B — Studio Glass

A producer found four long scratches on the inside of Studio B's glass partition. Security video shows the room empty for the entire night.

Item C — Missing Resident

Named resident Owen Pike left his apartment at 12:19 A.M. carrying only his keys. His car remained. A traffic camera later captured him walking toward the old river transmitter site.

Item D — Second Name

Another named resident, Dr. Celia Ward, received a voicemail at 12:07 A.M. containing only twelve seconds of breathing and the faint sound of the station's old sign-off music.

Witness Statements

INTERVIEW EXTRACTS

Mina Cross — Daughter of Daniel Cross

Mina says her father used the phrase 'keep the red light burning' only with his overnight crew. She believes somebody from the old station staff is responsible and immediately names Owen Pike.

Rafael Dunn — Current Engineer

Rafael says a dead host appearing only in the transmission archive could result from a routing fault, but not the scratches or the private references. He also reveals that the river transmitter still has emergency power.

Dr. Celia Ward — Named Resident

Ward initially denies knowing Cross. Later she admits she treated him after an accident fourteen years ago. She refuses to discuss who else was present.

Evan Rusk — Former Producer

Rusk says Cross, Pike, Ward, and he were together the night a teenage caller vanished after leaving the station. He insists the caller simply ran away and says Cross became obsessed with the case.

Locations

FIELD NOTES

WCRK Studio

Renovated three years ago. Studio B contains no exterior window and only one monitored door. The scratches appeared between routine inspections.

River Transmitter

A fenced, mostly automated facility beside the river. The original control room has been unused for years but retains emergency electricity and analog equipment.

Old Footbridge

Between the station and transmitter. Fourteen years ago, seventeen-year-old caller Lila Venn was last seen near the bridge after phoning Cross's overnight show.

Archive Documents

RESEARCH EXTRACTS

Archived Police Summary — Lila Venn

Lila called WCRK shortly before disappearing. Cross told police she sounded frightened and said she was being followed. Pike, Ward, Rusk, and Cross all gave statements that night, but no charges were filed.

Station Log — Fourteen Years Ago

A handwritten engineering log notes a power failure at 12:03 A.M. and a manual switch to the river transmitter. Beside it, Cross wrote: 'She was still on the line.'

Recent Probate Inventory

After Cross's death, Mina received boxes of tapes and notebooks. One notebook contains Cross's private investigation into Lila's disappearance—but several final pages have been carefully removed.

Traffic Record

Owen Pike's route toward the transmitter begins after the broadcast. No evidence places him at the station before midnight.

New Leads

SECOND-PASS REVIEW

1. Do not assume the dead man's voice proves the dead man is the active threat. Establish who benefits from using Cross's identity.

2. The scratches make a haunting plausible; the transmission-only recording makes a technical hoax plausible; the old disappearance gives several living people motive to lie.

3. Work out why Owen walks toward the transmitter AFTER the broadcast. If he created the message, his timeline requires an explanation.

4. The three named residents share an old event, but sharing a secret does not make all three equally responsible.

5. Before filing the report, answer two separate questions: what supernatural force is present, and who set tonight's events in motion?

Hunter's Call: Do not identify the threat from a single clue. Explain the complete chain of events before filing your report.
