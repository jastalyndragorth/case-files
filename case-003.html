Initial Field Briefing

CASE 003 • BELLWEATHER CLOCK & REPAIR • PRIORITY: REVIEW

Three people have reported losing approximately one hour after entering the same block of an aging downtown shopping district. None remembers being unconscious. All three insist they were somewhere else during the missing time.

The newest victim, Eli Mercer, was found sitting behind a locked clock-repair shop at 2:18 A.M. He had been reported missing less than ninety minutes earlier.

Complication: Eli insists he was gone for nearly eleven years.

According to Eli, he married, moved west, raised a daughter, and watched her start school. He can describe the house, his job, neighbors, birthdays, arguments, and hundreds of ordinary details. No evidence that any of it happened exists.

Hunter Objective: Determine what happened during the missing hour, identify the threat, and explain why the victims remember lives they never lived.

Evidence

RECOVERED MATERIAL • BELLWEATHER BLOCK

Item A — Security Video

At 1:21 A.M., Eli enters the alley beside Bellweather Clock & Repair. At 2:17 A.M., he appears again near the rear door and collapses. The camera records continuously. No missing hour exists on the footage.

Item B — Wrist Marks

Paramedics documented faint paired marks on Eli's forearm and unusual weakness inconsistent with his short walk through the alley. He does not remember being injured.

Item C — Stopped Watch

Eli's analog watch stopped at 1:17. A previous survivor's watch also stopped during her incident, but at 11:42 P.M.

Item D — Blue Residue

A thin blue-green residue was found on the rear service-door frame and again on a pipe leading toward the building's lower level. Police photographs classify it as old paint.

Witness Statements

INTERVIEW EXTRACTS

Eli Mercer — Survivor

“I know you think it was a dream. Dreams don't give you eleven years of mornings. My daughter's name was June. She hated peas. She had a scar on her chin from falling off her bike. I remember carrying her into the house.”

When shown current photographs, Eli recognizes everyone but becomes distressed that none have aged.

Mara Keene — First Reported Survivor

Six weeks ago, Mara was found asleep in her parked car two blocks away. She remembers spending “months” reconciled with her estranged sister. She initially blamed exhaustion and did not report the experience until hearing about Eli.

Officer Dana Holt

Holt found Eli. She says he repeatedly asked what year it was and begged her to call a wife whose name does not appear in any public record. Holt also noticed that every digital clock visible through the shop window displayed 1:17, although her patrol-car clock read 2:20.

Locations

FIELD MAP NOTES

Bellweather Clock & Repair

Closed for nine years after owner Arthur Bellweather died. The front showroom remains packed with clocks. The building has no active utility account, though neighbors report brief electrical flickers at night.

Rear Alley

Shared by four businesses. Eli entered from the south. The camera covering the alley belongs to a pharmacy and has no direct connection to the clock shop.

Lower Workroom

Original building plans show a basement repair room accessible by an interior stair and an old freight hatch. The current property manager says the hatch was permanently sealed during renovation. No recent inspection of the lower room is on file.

Archive Documents

RESEARCH EXTRACTS

1978 Newspaper Obituary

Arthur Bellweather's wife, Evelyn, died after a long illness. A later local story claims Arthur stopped every clock in the shop at the moment of her death. Contemporary photographs show the business open normally the following morning.

2017 Property Inspection

An inspector noted a recurring electrical fault that caused several digital displays to reset after brief power interruptions. The repair was deferred because the shop was already scheduled to close.

Undated Maintenance Photograph

A photograph taken inside the lower workroom shows a cot, bottled water, old restraints attached to a pipe, and discarded medical supplies. The image predates Eli's disappearance but was never included in the public property listing.

New Leads

RESEARCH PRIORITIES

1. Establish whether time actually stopped. The security camera, patrol-car clock, and outside witnesses all experienced an ordinary hour.

2. Separate the clock-shop legend from the current incidents. Arthur Bellweather's story may explain the location's reputation without explaining the victims.

3. Consult the Bestiary for creatures associated with altered perception, convincing alternate realities, incapacitated victims, feeding behavior, and waking confusion.

4. Ask why a supernatural phenomenon that appears psychological leaves physical evidence on its victims.

Hunter's Call: Decide what is real, what only felt real, and what evidence exists in both versions of events. Then file your theory.
